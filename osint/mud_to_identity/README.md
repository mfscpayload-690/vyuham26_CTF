## 1. Challenge Overview

A forensic geology and vehicle intelligence challenge. Operators were tasked with tracking a suspect or vehicle through environmental soil/mud spatter characteristics, tire tread patterns, and regional mineralogy profiles.

## 2. Geological & Geospatial Analysis

1. **Mineralogical Mapping:** Analyzed sediment coloration, clay content, and organic markers indicated in field logs.
2. **Vehicle Sourcing:** Correlated tire groove geometry and mud accumulation patterns against regional quarry and off-road trail registries.
3. **Identity Correlation:** Linked the vehicle license/registration data discovered at the target regional hub with public social registries, resolving the target operative's identity.
